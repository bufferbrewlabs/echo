Fast to open. Easy to trust.

Echo stays out of the way in your menu bar and shows up right where you’re typing.

⌘

Global shortcut

Defaults to ⌘⇧V and can be changed to any combination you like.

⌖

Appears at your caret

A compact, resizable popup opens near the text caret, falling back to your mouse position.

⌕

Search & filter

Search 300 recent clips instantly, or filter down to just text or images.

★

Pin what you reuse

Pinned clips stay at the top and survive normal history cleanup.

✎

Edit & alias

Edit a clip in place, save a fragment as a new clip, or give text a searchable alias.

◐

Native & accessible

Light and dark appearance, keyboard focus indicators and accessible controls.

Stays on your Mac.

Clips and images are stored locally on your Mac. Mark a text clip as hidden and Echo shows only its alias in the popup — it still pastes the full text.

Build it in a minute.

Requires macOS 13+ and Apple’s Swift toolchain (Xcode or Command Line Tools).

# clone, build, sign and install to /Applications
git clone https://github.com/bufferBrew/echo.git
cd echo
./build.sh
open /Applications/Echo.app

Automatic paste needs System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → Echo. Without it, Echo still copies the clip so you can press ⌘V.
